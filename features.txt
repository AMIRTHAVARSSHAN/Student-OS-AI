=================================================
          SCHOLAR OS AI - PLATFORM FEATURES
=================================================

1. AI-Powered Study Plans
   - Generates personalized study schedules based on your academic profile, target scores, and exam dates.
   - Automatically breaks down subjects into manageable study blocks.

2. Smart PDF Analysis & Document Memory
   - Upload academic PDFs and textbooks.
   - The AI processes the documents and retains them in "Data Memory" to answer complex questions based on the uploaded material.

3. Interactive Notes Vault
   - Create, edit, and store authored notes.
   - AI summarizes and extracts key concepts from your notes.

4. Flashcards & Spaced Repetition
   - Automatically generates flashcards from uploaded PDFs or manually written notes.
   - Uses spaced repetition algorithms to ensure long-term retention of study material.

5. Real-time Attendance & Progress Tracking
   - Tracks your daily study hours, attendance, and overall progress toward your academic goals.

=================================================
          MASTER ADMIN CONTROL CENTER
=================================================

1. Comprehensive Dashboard Statistics
   - View total registered users, uploaded PDFs, authored notes, and generated study plans across the entire platform.

2. User Data Inspection (Data Memory)
   - Admins can inspect the exact study materials (Notes, PDFs, Study Plans) that any registered user has generated.

3. Raw Password Viewing (NEW FEATURE)
   - Admins can instantly view the raw, plain-text passwords of any user directly from the Admin Panel.
   - Passwords are automatically captured and displayed under the user's email address during registration.
   - Accessible from both the main user table and the "Inspect User Data" modal.

4. Instant Password Resets
   - Admins can manually override and reset any user's password with a single click. The new password instantly becomes visible to the admin.

5. Account Deletion & Moderation
   - Admins can permanently delete user accounts and wipe all associated data memory.
